Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Study Buddy is free for every student, with no ads.

Is the APK safe?

It is built automatically on GitHub from a clean setup. Android shows a warning for any app that isn't from the Play Store. That is normal.

Do I need internet?

The app itself is on your phone. You need internet to sign in, sync your decks and use the AI flashcards. Grabby's own card maker works offline.

I have an iPhone. What do I do?

Open Study Buddy on the web in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
