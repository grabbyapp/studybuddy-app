How to install on Android

Takes less than 30 seconds — no Play Store account needed.

  1. Tap "Download for Android" above The StudyBuddy.apk file downloads directly right away. If Chrome asks to confirm, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open StudyBuddy.apk Tap Open on the download notification, or open your phone's Files / Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installing the app If Android asks for permission, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Study Buddy & meet Grabby! Create your free student account and start scanning notes. Future updates notify you right inside the app!
💡 Already have Study Buddy installed? Just install the new APK right over the old one — all your decks, streaks, XP, coins, and notes stay safely saved!

Quick Questions

Is Study Buddy really free?

Yes! Study Buddy is 100% free for students with zero ads and zero subscriptions.

Why does Android show a warning when installing an APK?

Android shows that message for every app downloaded outside Google Play. Study Buddy is packed cleanly with everything inside the APK so it works fast and offline.

Does it work without internet?

Yes! Your flashcards, quizzes, 60s Blitz, Pomodoro timer, 11 soundscapes, planner, notes, Grabby's offline brain, and Study Room all work 100% offline. Changes sync automatically once you reconnect.
