# Study Buddy for Android

Scan your notes into flashcards and quizzes, with a surprise study alarm and LET review. Free for every student.

## Download

**[Download StudyBuddy.apk](https://github.com/grabbyapp/studybuddy-app/releases/latest/download/StudyBuddy.apk)**

Or visit the download page: https://grabbyapp.github.io/studybuddy-app/

## How to install

1. Open the download link on your Android phone and download **StudyBuddy.apk**.
2. Open the file. If your phone blocks it, tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

The app always loads the newest version of Study Buddy, so you only need to install it once.

Prefer the browser? Use it on the web: https://melodic-mousse-4aeab3.netlify.app/
